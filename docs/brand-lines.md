# TWUN — scattered text stickers

Six short text-only marks per page, three in each unused outer gutter. No banners,
images, links, icons, secondary paragraphs, controls, timers or extra API calls.
Placement, type size, angle, color and horizontal offset differ per sticker and page.
These are brand statements, not prices, return claims or investment advice.

## Overview

- Think relative.
- Pick a side.
- Not just up. Against what?
- Two stocks. One view.
- Bring your conviction.
- Which stock wins your vote?

## News

- Read. Then react.
- Context beats noise.
- Question the hype.
- Headlines ≠ the whole story.
- Less noise. More signal.
- Form your own view.

## Trade

- Long or short?
- Against what?
- A thesis. Not a hunch.
- Two stocks. Your call.
- Mind the margin.
- Price the perspective.

## Portfolio

- Own the outcome.
- Beyond the green.
- Track the thesis.
- Conviction meets numbers.
- Know your exposure.
- The whole picture.

## Placement

No main-column width or page grid changes. Overview / News appear at >=1760px,
Trade / Portfolio at >=2200px; minimum viewport height 700px. Overview stickers
are anchored inside the hero and scroll away before the full-width stock ticker.
Other pages use staggered fixed gutter positions; chart expansion and modals remain
above them. All stickers have pointer-events:none and are decorative (aria-hidden).
Angles and positions are deterministic, not random on every render. Old poster
dismissal storage is not read, removed or reused. Printing hides all stickers.
